We've reached the end of volume 2 of the L/L Research Channeling Archives books, and on the last day of the month. Although this session contains a lot of inaudible parts, I think what comes through is quite profound the more I read it. I'm particularly struck by the emphasis those of Latuii place on sensation, namely the direction to provide it _attention_ and to kind of _relax_ into it. Much of it rhymes with the topics beings pursued by the Solar Cross Foundation in my read-through of _Star Wards_ with respect to the mysterious "tensor equations" they've hinted at in the past. Hopefully tomorrow we can dig into these a bit because they appear to articulate a slightly different method for dealing with the permeability of consciousness that Latuii addresses here.

In this session, the focus falls less on technique than on awareness of the operation of consciousness. If all is one and all is love, then what is it that stimulates, inspires, and disturbs us arising from without and impinging upon the "self" we cling to -- that self mostly simply defined as the part of the creation within the boundaries of our skin? We can speak of the material effects and material goals, but Latuii reminds us that these are running dogs for much deeper forces at work. In fact, they seem to suggest that the conduct of an individual mind/body/spirit complex within the veiled illusion is almost a kind of trick, a means by which we can experience the infinity of love's forms not as a way to distract us from the underlying love so much as a way to experience it counter-intellectually, viscerally, and emotionally.

<!-- more -->

This is another reason why meditation is so salutary to the seeker's awakening to this underlying love: it places the intellect in the proper relation to it. The immediacy of experience conveys aspects of love that perhaps do not stand out when holistically perceived; only in the light of the moon, so to speak, do certain qualities come into view. However, it is the love that is the subject, not the illusory distortion that brings this love into novel recognition. Much of what I'll be trying to dig into in the next few days or weeks revolves around techniques and approaches with which one can work with the radical novelty of love's full spectrum filtered into the illusion, and so perhaps this couldn't be a better introduction. 

However, keep in mind the core thesis: _it is all love_, and it is for that reason that we stand as gatekeepers to its ingress and egress. Every sensation we receive bears the hallmarks of this love; every reaction or response we issue reflects our recognition of this love. And when we go about attaching importance to the details of this playing out in our lives, we are perhaps attempting more and more to cultivate the awareness with which we apprehend that love rather than simply getting better at saying "yes" to nice things and "no" to unpleasant things.
