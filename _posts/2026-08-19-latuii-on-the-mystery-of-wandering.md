I recently talked on my podcast, [_(inaudible)_](https://inaudible.show/episode-70/), about the inscrutable paradox of one of the core concepts in the messages from the Confederation: that of being a mind/body/spirit complex with free will _while also_ aspiring to be an instrument of the Creator. The question doesn't so much center around whether "we" have free will or not, but more of what "we" are in the first place. The entire crux of the issue of will lies in the source of its agency, and if the self is not understood completely then what it means for it to exercise choice is uncertain, to put it mildly!

Here's a short excerpt from the same 1980 session as last post hinting at how this works for wanderers, e.g. those from higher densities who assume the veil for the purposes of incarnating into third density to serve and grow without conscious awareness of their pedigree. The concept of the wanderer doesn't really make sense at all unless there is more to the self than its conscious side. This is why meditation is the best training for service: it gets you in touch with this side such that you can make free will decisions that act upon information you cannot possibly obtain consciously (hunches, intuitions, past life memories) while also helping you use the third density experience to further learn about yourself and make polarizing choices.

<!-- more -->

Note also that _simply accepting one's feeling of displacement_ is important for the wanderer. I wonder if this confirmation and comfort from complexes like Latuii isn't a signficant part of a wanderer's "activation." Imagine being a plumber and not knowing it; everybody's coming to you with all these problems and you have no idea why. Somebody tells you you are a plumber, you have the training, you just don't remember. It's not necessarily good news, but it at least explains what was once pure chaos. And who knows? You might pick up that wrench and something comes back to you…

> You are coming in contact with your teacher, who has been dealing with you for some time. Your lessons do not have any connection with your physical life in this density, at this time, rather, with the continuing evolution of your true self in its whole capacity. This part of your education is something that you could not do without. Nor could most of those who are called “apples” or “wanderers” by this instrument. The vibrations of your adopted dwelling place are such that it is very difficult for most of you to receive consciously the continuing lessons that you desire in your spirit self. Therefore, they must take place when your spirit is released through sleep.
