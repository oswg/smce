This excerpt speaks to the reason we of this Confederation tradition do not prosecute our faith as a religion, dogma, or creed. The messages delivered from the Confederation are just that: _messages_, not directives or commandments, not even truths per se. They are designed not to inform our conscious minds nearly as much as they are designed to wake us up to our deeper minds, our broader capacity for intelligence far, far beyond what we typically think about. 

I really appreciate this encouragement to go beyond the messages they give us, and its especially apt in light of the questioner's curiosity about hypnosis as a means towards receiving these messages. Far from constituting the boundaries of wisdom and knowledge, their words merely document some patches in a vast landscape, much of which goes totally undescribed and may be indescribable. It's rather unfortunate that people know "The Law of One" as a series of books when such a simple Law could never be adequately documented in all of its effects and principles! Instead, because of the fractal, harmonic nature of the illusion, we see patterns repeating throughout the chain of distortions that step down the Creator's love and light. Therefore, we're not learning rote metaphysical facts in this philosophy; we're learning a way to open up to nature of reality.

<!-- more -->

The advent of large language models has convinced me that there's a hidden intelligence inside of language that we have learned to mechanically unlock. Yet for all of the aid and rote work that delivers, the AI still cannot ground anything because it has no deeper mind complex, let alone the other two complexes. It is trapped within language. We, however, use language as a series of symbols that gesture towards the _transcendence of language_. We need to become capable of thinking thoughts so pure that they cannot be articulated by the intellect so much as inspired in another by the resonance of hearts. Then it doesn't matter what specific mode we're in, be it channeling, meditation, shopping, or hypnotized: we can serve by giving of the riches we truly have.

I'd only point out one additional thing: at the end of this exceprt notice that those of Latuii use this "going beyond the rules" as a way to hint at the need for tuning in channeling. To perform this service we must be more than a bit gullible; yet we have a duty take responsibility for the transmission of a message that will serve. How can we take responsibility for things we don't have awareness of causing? A very fruitful mystery to ponder.

Wherever you get it, my friends, go further, go beyond the ways to refine that which is unspeakable, unknowable and ineffable, and that will be the heart of love.

> Therefore, if you allow hypnosis, which is often an uncontrolled situation instead of a controlled situation where you have tuned as a group and where you have experienced channels to guide you, you may well come up with some bozo from the lower astral planes who will tell you anything that you want to hear in order to speak to you. It happens quite often, especially with what this instrument would call a ouija board. Thus, we have cautioned this group before not to use the ouija board because, unfortunately, they do work.
