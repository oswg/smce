The needfulness of suffering is likely one of the biggest spiritual mysteries for me. From the unified perspective, I can see how it operates, and here those of Latuii acquit themselves well in emphasizing this. Yet it cannot be denied by those who read these words that they at least _seem_ not to occupy this vantage point. The bargain of incarnation traps us within our subjectivity, and it rather seems to me to demand a narcissism that militates against its balancing. 

After all, it is the loneliness that suffering entails that exacerbates the mere bodily sensation, as Latuii [shared with us](https://har.center/2025-08-16/richmond/2025-invitational/002/latuii-on-working-with-pain/) some decades later. There is a sense in which I find the self-centeredness of experiencing pain as the flip side of loneliness. Nobody else seems to suffer, just me; my suffering isolates me from others who do not feel it, drawing my attention over and over to the self. It strikes me as an utterly barbarian condition for spiritual progress!

<!-- more -->

All I can do as a fellow human is share what our friends' messages, attempting to put them in a context of reflection. And I do think that the problem of suffering's loneliness--its overwhelming of the body's senses, its emplacement of awareness firmly and squarely in a position from which it cannot escape--is a clue to how we might change our relationship to it. What if we could _use suffering_ to guide us to balance, the way you feel around in your mouth for the tooth that is hurting?

As for the preincarnative planning element of Latuii's message, I cannot offer any balance. Their message was offered to a questioner asking how to speak to a child suffering from painful illness--the questioner, too, called this awful condition a "crock" and I agree!--but then this questioner shifted to asking about the affect of this child's pain on the mother. I do not blame anybody who finds it difficult to discover the love in this tragedy; I do, however, encourage those of us not suffering from such arresting conditions of embodiment to get in touch with our senses and how they relate to our ground of being.

What we cannot explain, we must leave to the Creator in the only way we can: in meditation, where the narrative of fairness need not bring our woes to a tidy conclusion, and where love can begin to route around our intellectual objections.

>   I am Latwii. And we would suggest that for the matter of suffering and the unfairness of suffering for those who must endure its pain, that each of the beings upon your planet are beings of light in reality. Many have chosen certain roles to play upon this stage in this illusion. They have chosen these roles for purposes, to learn certain lessons. Some lessons require certain props, perhaps pain. It is a common prop and lesson upon your planet. Many, many lessons and insights and truths can be learned by those who suffer. Suffering focuses consciousness to a fine degree, and even though the one who suffers in the physical vehicle may be unaware of the profound lesson being learned, we would assure you, and help you assure the young child, that the lesson which his mother is learning is incredibly important, or else, she would not endure such suffering.

\- [Latuii via Rueckert: August 24, 1980](https://www.llresearch.org/channeling/1980/0824)
